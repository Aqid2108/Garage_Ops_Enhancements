# Folder Structure

The three Word documents at each project root remain the source of truth for the overall project:

1. Project timeline
2. Hardware components report
3. General job profiles

The `job-profiles/` folder breaks those general documents into focused working areas. Every role contains:

- `README.md` — role purpose, boundary and starting steps
- `weekly-tasks.md` — assigned work by active period
- `hardware-and-interfaces.md` — components, voltage/signals and ownership
- `testing-and-evidence.md` — required proof of completion
- `component-and-voltage-register.md` — included for roles that make electrical or component decisions
- `work/` — role-specific locations for editable files and evidence

Do not delete the general Word documents after members begin using the role folders.
