## Required design notes

- State the voltage and expected current at every external port.
- Identify 3.3 V, 5 V, motor, servo and battery domains clearly.
- Record level shifting, reverse-polarity protection, fuses and current limits.
- Confirm the continuous and peak rating of each motor or servo driver.
- Do not release a PCB while any required electrical rating remains unknown.
