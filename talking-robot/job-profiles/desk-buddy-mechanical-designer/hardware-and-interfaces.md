# Hardware and Interfaces

The values below identify the electrical domain the member will encounter. **TBC** means the exact component or rating must be confirmed from the selected part's datasheet before connection or purchase.

| Component / interface | Voltage or signal domain | What this role must do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display / touchscreen | Mechanical interface | Record active area, bezel, mounting points and cable bend clearance. |
| Microphone and speakers | Mechanical/acoustic interface | Avoid blocking pickup; provide speaker openings and isolation. |
| Raspberry Pi and cooling | Maintain airflow | Provide mounting and service access. |
| STS3215 mechanism | Dedicated cable path and safe travel envelope | Provide hard-stop clearance and avoid pinch points. |
| Serial servo board and power hardware | Keep terminals protected | Provide mounting, airflow and accessible connectors. |

## Checks before work begins

- [ ] Desktop base remains stable through the full servo motion.
- [ ] Display, microphone and speaker openings are aligned.
- [ ] Ventilation is not blocked.
- [ ] Servo motion cannot pinch cables or fingers.
- [ ] The enclosure can be opened for maintenance.
